Black Pearl

One ship, one lantern, and a sea with no end.

Turn your phone sideways for a wider sea.

How to play

  • Steer across the sea. Drag up and down anywhere, or use W / S or the arrow keys. The Pearl never stops.

  • Cursed Aztec gold feeds your lantern. The lantern burns down slowly. When night falls, it is also your only light.

  • Wrecks, reefs and tentacles hurt. Jack the Monkey warns you with a dashed line on the water just before he leaps. Lily pads only slow you down.

  • Rum barrel blocks one hit.

  • The Kraken carries you through everything for a few seconds.

  • Davy Jones' heart widens your light and pulls nearby treasure toward you.
